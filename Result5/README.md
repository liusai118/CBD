# LGE-GNN-Guided Identification of the NRF2 Pathway as a Key Mediator of CBD's Neuroprotective Effects

## Overview
In this section, we identify NRF2 as the central mediator of CBD’s neuroprotective effects in ischemia/reperfusion (I/R) injury.  
LGE-GNN–guided predictions combined with single-cell RNA sequencing (scRNA-seq) analyses implicate NRF2 activation as a critical mechanism underlying CBD-mediated neuroprotection.
