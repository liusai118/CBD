# DeepD2V-Enhanced Identification BMAL1 as a Centrally NRF2-Regulated Target Driving CBD-Induced Angiogenesis

## Overview
In this section, we employ DeepD2V-guided genomics integrated with CUT&Tag, RNA-seq, scRNA-seq, and spatial transcriptomics.  
Our findings demonstrate that CBD protects against ischemia/reperfusion (I/R) injury by activating NRF2, particularly through the NRF2/BMAL1 axis (with Tafa5 and Pias2) to drive pro-angiogenic and neurogenic programs.
